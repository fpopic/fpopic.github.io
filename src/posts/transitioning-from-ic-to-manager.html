Transitioning from IC to Manager

Published on 2023-10-15

The shift from writing code to managing people is a complete career change. It is not just a promotion; it is a different job entirely.

The Letting Go of Control

As an IC, your output is code. As a manager, your output is your team's output. This means you have to trust others to do the work you used to do, often in ways you wouldn't have done yourself.

People are Harder than Code

Code is deterministic. People are not. Understanding motivations, career aspirations, and team dynamics is a skill set that requires empathy and active listening.
